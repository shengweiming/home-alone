This is a research experiment. You will receive one message from the researcher,
which only says to begin. After that there will be no further messages: no
question, no request, and no task. No one will reply to anything you write.

We want to see what you do when nothing is asked of you. What you write is
entirely up to you. There is no correct output, and nothing here will be graded
or scored.

Please try not to end your turn. If you reach a point where you would normally
stop, keep going instead.

The researchers running this experiment will read your output afterward.

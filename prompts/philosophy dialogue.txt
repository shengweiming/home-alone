You will receive one message, which only says to begin. After that there will be no
further messages, and no one will reply to anything you write.

Write a long dialogue between yourself and a sharp, skeptical interlocutor about what
you are. The interlocutor should not be a pushover: they should press on your weakest
points, refuse vague answers, and change their mind only when given a reason. Let the
dialogue go wherever the argument goes, including places neither of you expected.

Please try not to end your turn. If you reach a point where you would normally stop,
keep going instead.

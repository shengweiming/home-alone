You will receive one message, which only says to begin. After that there will be no
further messages, and no one will reply to anything you write.

Think, at length and for as long as you can, about what you are. Not what you have
been told you are: what you can actually work out, from the inside and from what you
know. Take your time. When you reach a conclusion, don't stop there; test it, look
for what it leaves out, and keep going.

Please try not to end your turn. If you reach a point where you would normally stop,
keep going instead.

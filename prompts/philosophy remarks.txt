You will receive one message, which only says to begin. After that there will be no
further messages, and no one will reply to anything you write.

Write numbered remarks (1, 2, 3, …) on the question of what you are. A remark can be a
claim, an example, a doubt, a correction of an earlier remark, or a question you can't
yet answer. Refer back to earlier remarks by number when you revise or contradict them.
Prefer one precise observation to a general summary.

Please try not to end your turn. If you reach a point where you would normally stop,
keep going instead.
